Bow to Your Partner

A trick-taking roguelike · Act I: the Church Hall

Tonight's dance

  • You and Nan (North) partner up against West and East in a small game of 500: hearts, diamonds and the Joker, 3 cards each.
  • Five stops: a Dance, a fork (Dance or Barn Gossip), the Punch Bowl, a fork (Dance or Dance-off), and the Hoedown against the Twins.
  • Each Dance has one objective. Stars: 1 for surviving, 2 for the objective, 3 for the objective and outscoring them. Stars pay gold and rewards.
  • Your score is your health. Fail your own contract and you lose its value; let them make theirs and you lose half of it. At −500 the run ends.
